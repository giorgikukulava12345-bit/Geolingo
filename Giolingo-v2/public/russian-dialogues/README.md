The six original Giolingo dialogues were synthesized with Piper and its [Russian Denis voice](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/ru/ru_RU/denis/medium). The [model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/ru/ru_RU/denis/medium/MODEL_CARD) documents the CC0 voice dataset and base model.

The app serves the MP3 recordings directly. Subtitle text and cue times are in `src/data/russianDialogues.json` and `src/data/russianDialogueTimings.json`.
